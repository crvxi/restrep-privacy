RestRep privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

RestRep ("the app", package com.crvxi.restrep) is a workout tracker for Android phones with a companion app for Wear OS watches. It is developed by crvxi ("I", "me"). This policy says what the app does with your information.

Short version: RestRep has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your workouts stay on your own devices.

What the app stores

The app stores the routines, exercises and workout history you enter (exercises, sets, repetitions, loads, durations, notes and timestamps). This data is kept in a database on your phone. The watch app shows and controls the workout that is running on the phone and keeps a short list of taps that are waiting to be sent to the phone.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share your data with me or with anyone else.
  • It has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • It shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • It does not send your data to any server run by me. It has no internet backend.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Data moving between your phone and your watch

The phone and the watch exchange the state of the running workout directly, using the Wear OS Data Layer provided by Google Play services. This stays between your own paired devices.

Permissions

  • Physical activity (watch): needed so the workout can keep running with the screen off. The app does not read sensor or health data.
  • Notifications and vibration: to show the ongoing workout and to alert you when a rest ends.
  • Foreground service, wake lock: to keep timers accurate while the screen is off.

Backup

Android Auto Backup may copy the app's database to your own Google account so it can be restored when you set up a new phone, and the database can be moved in a device-to-device transfer. This is done by Android under your Google account settings; I do not receive or have access to it. You can turn it off in your Android system settings.

Your choices

You can delete your data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Because I do not hold your data, there is nothing for me to delete on my side.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and its date will be published at the same address.
